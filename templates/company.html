About Us

Located in Dongguan with easy access to Hong Kong, we are a professional silicone & rubber manufacturer equipped with modern production lines and an experienced technical team.

Annual capacity: nearly 1,000 tons. We use eco-friendly materials to produce custom parts with excellent resistance to temperature, chemicals, weathering, oil, abrasion, tearing, and corrosion — plus adjustable hardness.

ISO9001 certified, with products available to meet FDA and RoHS standards.

One-stop service: Design → Sampling → Quotation → Mass Production.
