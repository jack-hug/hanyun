Liquid Silicone Rubber Injection Molding

Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR) injection molding is a high-precision manufacturing process that produces complex silicone components with exceptional consistency and quality. Our advanced LSR injection molding capabilities enable us to create intricate geometries with tight tolerances, making it ideal for applications requiring durability, flexibility, and biocompatibility.

Key Advantages:
  • High-volume production with consistent quality
  • Complex geometry and tight tolerance capabilities
  • Excellent heat resistance and chemical stability
  • FDA-compliant materials for medical and food applications

Silicone Rubber Compression Molding

With decades of industry expertise, our silicone rubber compression molding service delivers premium solid silicone components for a wide range of applications. This versatile manufacturing process is ideal for producing durable, high-performance parts with excellent mechanical properties, making it a cost-effective solution for both prototyping and mass production.

Key Advantages:
  • Cost-effective for small to medium production runs
  • Excellent material strength and durability
  • Ideal for thick-walled and large components
  • Wide material selection for diverse applications

Liquid Silicone Rubber Overmolding

Liquid Silicone Rubber overmolding, also known as two-shot molding, is an advanced manufacturing technique that bonds liquid silicone directly onto plastic or metal substrates. This innovative process enhances product performance by creating a seamless, integrated component with superior sealing, comfort, and aesthetic properties, while simplifying assembly and reducing production costs.

Key Advantages:
  • Superior waterproof and dustproof sealing performance
  • Enhanced product ergonomics and comfort
  • Eliminates secondary assembly operations
  • Excellent chemical and environmental resistance
